Game Rules Cheat Sheets

Catan

3-4 Players 60-90 Min

Setup

  • Resources: Place terrain hexes randomly or use standard map.
  • Numbers: Distribute number tokens alphabetically skipping 'I'.
  • Initial: Roll for order. Place 1 settlement + 1 road. Repeat in reverse order for 2nd settlement.
  • Starting Income: Take 1 resource card for each hex adjacent to your 2nd settlement.

Turn Sequence

  • 1. Roll Dice: All players collect resources matched to the hex number rolled.
  • 2. Trade: Trade resource cards with other players or with the bank (4:1 or port rate).
  • 3. Build: Spend resources on Roads, Settlements, Cities, or Dev Cards.

Key Rules

  • Distance Rule: Settlements must be at least 2 intersections away from all others.
  • Rolling a 7: Discard half cards if holding >7. Move the Robber to block income. Steal 1 card.
  • Victory: First player to reach 10 Victory Points on their own turn wins.

Carcassonne

2-5 Players 35 Min

Turn Sequence

  • 1. Draw: Draw exactly one land tile from the pool.
  • 2. Place: Attach it to existing tiles matching all adjacent features (Road, City, Field).
  • 3. Meeple (Optional): Place a worker from your supply onto a feature on that newly placed tile.
  • 4. Score: Check if any features are completed. Score points and return meeples.

Scoring Rules

  • Roads: 1 Point per tile when capped at both ends.
  • Cities: 2 Points per tile + 2 per shield emblem when fully enclosed.
  • Monasteries: 9 Points when completely surrounded by 8 tiles.
  • Farmers: 3 Points per completed city adjacent to your field (scored only at endgame).

Restrictions

  • No Sharing: Cannot place a meeple on a feature already occupied by another player's meeple.
  • Merging: Features can merge later through strategic tile placement. Majority rules for score.

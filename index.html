Condensed Game Rules

Brass Lancashire (2P)

2 Players 90-120 Min

Game Overview

  • Theme: Competing entrepreneurs expanding industrial empires in Lancashire during England's Industrial Revolution (1770–1870).
  • Goal: Highest Victory Points (VPs) at the end of the Rail Era wins.
  • Scoring: VPs come from Link tiles and flipped Industry tiles at the end of each era. At the end of the Rail Era only, convert cash to VPs: £10 = 1 VP.
  • Tie-Breakers: Highest income, then most remaining cash, then shared victory.

General Setup

  • 1. Map: Use Alternate Map (upper-left exclamation icon).
  • 2. Cards: Remove all cards without a cog icon (leaves 40 cards).
  • 3. Deck Tile: Place the Deck tile near the bottom of the draw deck.
    • Canal Era: Place "Stephenson’s Rocket" side up above the bottom 2 cards (1 per player).
    • Rail Era: Place Nathaniel Rothschild side up above the bottom 4 cards (2 per player).
  • 4. Distant Market: Remove tiles without a left-side cog (leaves one "-1", one "-3", two "0"). Place Marker on top space.
  • 5. Resource Markets: Fill all Coal and Iron spaces with cubes.

Player Setup

  • 1. Components: 1 Player Mat, 1 Character tile, 14 Links, 37 Industries (stacked by level).
  • 2. Markers: VP marker on "0"; Income marker on "10".
  • 3. Starting Hand: Draw 8 cards.
  • 4. Starting Cash: Take £25 from the bank.
  • 5. Turn Order: Shuffle Character tiles randomly.

Game/Round Structure

  • Eras: Each Era (Canal and Rail) lasts until all cards in hands and the deck are spent.
  • Player Turns: Perform 2 actions (Exception: 1 action in the very first round of the Canal Era).
  • Action Cost: Discard 1 card face-up per action, including passing.
  • End of Round Phase:
    • Adjust Turn Order: Lowest cash spender goes first; ties keep relative order.
    • Take Income: Collect track value from bank (or pay if negative).
    • Refill Hand: Draw back up to 8 cards.

Available Actions

  • Build: Discard a matching card to place the lowest-level industry from your mat.
    • Location Card: Build any industry at that named location (network not required).
    • Industry Card: Build that specific industry inside your network.
    • Double Action Wild: Spend both actions and discard any 2 cards to build any industry anywhere.
    • Era Icons: Respect all era restrictions printed on the player mats (indicated by colored half-moon icons).
    • First Industry Tile: If you have no tiles on board, you must use a Location Card or Double Wild.
    • Overbuilding: Replace any tile with a higher-level tile of the same type.
      • Own Tile: Allowed anytime; old cubes on it are lost.
      • Opponent Tile: Only Coal/Iron; allowed only if that resource is completely empty on the board and markets.
  • Network: Discard any card to place a Link tile adjacent to your existing network.
    • Canal Era: Pay £3. Max 1 link per line (Canal line only).
    • Rail Era: Pay £5 for 1 link, or £15 for 2 links (Rail line only). Consume 1 coal per link.
  • Develop: Discard any card to remove 1 or 2 lowest-level industry tiles from your mat. Consume 1 iron per tile.
  • Sell: Discard any card to flip one or more built Cotton Mills connected to a buyer.
    • To a Port: Trace a connected route to any player's unflipped Port. Flip both tiles; both players may gain income.
    • To Distant Market: Trace a connected route to a market trade icon (Ports/Border cities). Flip top market tile and advance the track marker.
      • If it hits "X", the sale fails: your mill stays unflipped and no income is gained.
      • Otherwise, flip your Cotton Mill and advance your Income marker by both the tile value and the market track reward.
  • Loan: Discard any card. Take £10, £20, or £30. Move income marker back 1, 2, or 3 levels (not individual spaces).
    • Restriction: You cannot take a loan during the final 4 rounds of the Rail Era (once the draw deck is empty).
    • Reminder: Revealing the Nathaniel Rothschild tile signals that the current round is your final opportunity to take a loan.
  • Pass: Discard 1 card. Do nothing.

Key Concepts

  • Networks & Connections:
    • Networks: Locations containing your industry tiles or adjacent to your link tiles.
    • Connections: Locations linked by an uninterrupted line of links belonging to any player.
  • Consuming Resources:
    • Iron: No connection required. Take free from any player's unflipped Iron Works first, then buy from market.
    • Coal: Requires a connection to the source. Take free from the closest connected unflipped mine first, then buy from market via a market connection.
  • Market Sales:
    • Trigger: Happens instantly when building an Iron Works, or a Coal Mine connected to a market icon.
    • Process: Move cubes from the tile to fill empty market track slots (filling expensive slots first). Collect cash shown next to the filled slots.
    • Trigger: If emptied, flip the tile immediately for income.
    • Restriction: This is automatic at build time; you cannot manually "Sell" resource cubes later. Market cash does not offset your turn expenditure for turn order.
  • Flipping Tiles: Tiles flip to their black side when emptied (mines), traded (mills/ports), or instantly when placed on the board (shipyards). Flipped tiles grant VPs at the end of the era.

Era Transitions & Restrictions

  • Canal Era Restrictions:
  • Max 1 industry tile per player per location.
  • Level 1 industries cannot be overbuilt. Use the Develop action to skip them.
  • Rail Era Transition:
  • Wipe Board: Remove all Level 1 industry tiles from the board to the box. Level 2+ remain.
  • Reset Market: Reset Distant Cotton Marker to top; shuffle all market tiles face down.
  • Rest Deck: Combine cards under the Deck tile with all discard piles; shuffle fully.
  • Draw Hands: Both players draw 8 cards.
  • Insert Deck Tile: Flip tile to Nathaniel Rothschild side; place above the bottom 4 cards (2 per player).
  • Rail Era Restrictions:
  • Multiple industries per player per location are allowed.
  • Final Scoring Clarification: Only flipped industry tiles score VPs. Any unflipped industries left on the board at the end of the game are worth 0 VPs.

Carcassonne

2-5 Players 35 Min

Turn Sequence

  • 1. Draw: Draw exactly one land tile from the pool.
  • 2. Place: Attach it to existing tiles matching all adjacent features (Road, City, Field).
  • 3. Meeple (Optional): Place a worker from your supply onto a feature on that newly placed tile.
  • 4. Score: Check if any features are completed. Score points and return meeples.

Scoring Rules

  • Roads: 1 Point per tile when capped at both ends.
  • Cities: 2 Points per tile + 2 per shield emblem when fully enclosed.
  • Monasteries: 9 Points when completely surrounded by 8 tiles.
  • Farmers: 3 Points per completed city adjacent to your field (scored only at endgame).

Restrictions

  • No Sharing: Cannot place a meeple on a feature already occupied by another player's meeple.
  • Merging: Features can merge later through strategic tile placement. Majority rules for score.
